Who Is Bless America

BLESS is a meme token created on the BNB Chain on July 4th, 2021, and has since been CTO'd (community takeover) by an amazing community who carry it forward together.

Born on Independence Day and carried by faith, community, and a shared belief in something bigger than the charts, Bless America stands for pride, unity, and blessings for everyone who holds it.
